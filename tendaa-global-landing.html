The problem

Two sides, one missing bridge.

Cross-border partnerships fail for a simple reason: neither side can verify the other. Tendaa Global exists to close that gap.

For global brands

Entering African markets means betting on distributors, retailers, and manufacturers you can't easily vet from abroad. Due diligence takes months, and a wrong pick can cost a market entirely.

For African brands

Reaching serious international partners means competing for attention with no track record abroad and no clear channel to be discovered by the people making the decisions.
